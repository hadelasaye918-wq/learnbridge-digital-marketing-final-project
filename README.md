# LearnBridge Academy — Digital Marketing Final Internship Project

## Five-Week Digital Marketing Internship Final Project

This project demonstrates the practical application of digital marketing skills developed during a five-week internship.

### Project Focus

- Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- Google Algorithms
- Social Media Marketing
- Content Marketing
- Google Ads
- Lead Generation
- Digital Marketing Strategy
- Corporate and Professional Development

### Project Objective

To develop an integrated digital marketing campaign for LearnBridge Academy, a fictional skills-training academy targeting students, graduates, and early-career professionals.

### Campaign Funnel

Awareness → Website Visit → Program Interest → Lead Form → Follow-up → Enrollment

### Project Files

- `index.html` — Campaign landing page
- `styles.css` — Website styling
- `script.js` — Lead-form interaction
- `campaign-plan.md` — Digital marketing campaign strategy
- `internship-final-report.md` — Final internship report

### Internship Learning Connection

**Week 1:** Introduction to SEO and Google Algorithms  
**Week 2:** Digital Marketing Strategy and Audience Research  
**Week 3:** Social Media and Campaign Planning  
**Week 4:** Google Ads and Digital Marketing Opportunities  
**Week 5:** Success Ladder to the Corporate World
