# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This final project presents a practical digital marketing campaign developed as part of a five-week Digital Marketing Internship.

The project, titled **"Digital Growth Campaign for LearnBridge Academy,"** was developed to demonstrate the practical application of digital marketing skills learned throughout the internship.

LearnBridge Academy is a fictional skills-training organization created for this project. It focuses on providing practical digital and career-development training to students, recent graduates, job seekers, and early-career professionals.

The project combines several digital marketing activities, including Search Engine Optimization (SEO), social media marketing, content marketing, Google Ads, lead generation, audience targeting, and performance measurement.

The campaign is designed around a simple digital marketing funnel:

**Awareness → Website Visit → Interest → Lead → Follow-up → Enrollment**

The final project includes a responsive campaign landing page, digital marketing campaign plan, lead-generation form, SEO strategy, social media strategy, paid advertising concepts, key performance indicators, and this final report.

The project demonstrates that digital marketing is most effective when different channels work together toward a clear organizational objective.
